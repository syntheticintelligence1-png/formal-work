**New terms** (defined before use):

- **Orphan supply** `Ω := S − Σ_u balanceOf(u)`: pool supply that no account owns.
- **Denormal product**: a DFP value whose magnitude is `m < 1e9`, outside the normalised range `[1e9, 1e18]` (L16875).
- **Epoch-reset window**: the set of losses `L < S` for which `_notifyLoss` produces `scale = 0`.
- **Seniority**: fToken holds a 1.0 claim (`fNav ≡ 1`, L7997) ranking ahead of xToken's residual claim `B·p − F`.

## 3. Action footprints (summary)

### Treasury

- Mutators other than `harvest` require roles: FX_MARKET for mint and redeem, R2 for `settle`, PROTOCOL_INITIALIZER, and DEFAULT_ADMIN for setters.
- The market is the only FX_MARKET holder and holds `nonReentrant`.
- `TreasuryV2.harvest` (L9648) and `TWFC.harvest` (L17409) are **open**.
- `TWFC.harvest` returns early in three cases:
  - the snapshot was taken in this block (L17411);
  - **`!isValid`** (L17415);
  - xNav = 0.

  Otherwise it charges `B −= U(r)` (L17422), captures the snapshot (L17423) and distributes.
- TWFC state loads with `_deductFundingCost` deduct the accrued funding **virtually** (L17477–L17481).
- Mint and redeem persist `B := _state.baseSupply ± …` (L9562, L9581, L9617).

### Market

- `mintFToken` (L7187), `mintXToken` (L7237), `redeemFToken` (L7283), `redeemXToken` (L7343) are all `nonReentrant`.
- MWFC runs `treasury.harvest()` first, through the `harvestFundingCost` modifier (L17187–L17195).

### Pools

- Only `checkpoint`, `claim(a, r)` and `claimHistorical` hold MRCA's lock (L16978, L16994, L17004, L17017). `deposit`, `withdraw` and `liquidate` are **unlocked**.
- `_checkpoint(u)` (L16298 / L15262) runs, in order:
  1. The gauge mint and **`_notifyReward(fxn, minted)` before the accrual** (L16301–L16306; Shareable L15264–L15269).
  2. Shareable only: `veHelper.checkpoint`.
  3. `_distributePendingReward` (L3739).
  4. `_updateSnapshot`. For FXN this applies the boost and re-notifies the unboosted remainder (L16348 / L15304).
  5. Compound the user's balance.
  6. Write `boostCheckpoint`: **Boostable stores `numTotalSupplyHistory`** (L16315); Shareable stores `numTotalSupplyHistory − 1` (L15490).
- `liquidate` (R2): `_checkpoint(0)`, then caches the fToken balance across the market call, then `_accumulateReward` at the old S, then `_notifyLoss`.
- `_notifyLoss(L)` (L16381 / L15496):
  - if `L ≥ S`: `x = 1e18`, `S = 0`, `err = 0`;
  - else: `x = ⌊(L·1e18 − err)/S⌋ + 1`, `err = x·S − (L·1e18 − err)`, `S −= L`.

  Then `prod := DFP.mul(prod, 1e18 − x)`.

**Who can poke.**

| Who | Actions |
|---|---|
| Anyone | TreasuryV2 and TWFC `harvest`; every MWFC action; pool `checkpoint(account)`, `claim(account)`, `deposit(·, receiver)` |
| R2 | `settle`, `liquidate` |
| R3 | `depositReward` (also reachable from `harvest` → splitter [A12]) |

The EMA is sampled by every mint, redeem, harvest and settle (`atlas/A2`).

## 6. Abstract transition system

```
S   = T × Mk × Π_pools P × Env
T   = (B, sU, ema, snapshot, 𝒫_T);   Mk = 𝒫_M
P   = (S, prod, err, Hist, per-user (b, prod, t, boostCkpt), per-token (I_τ[epoch‖exp], rewardData),
       per-user-token (pending, I_u), [Shareable: owner aggregates, H_o, owner(·)])
Env = (now, oracle answer, rate r, borrow index I, ve, gauge, external balances)
S0  = post-initialization states
Act = users {mint, redeem, harvest, deposit, withdraw, claim, checkpoint, accept, reject}
      ∪ R2 {liquidate, settle} ∪ R3 {depositReward, registry} ∪ R4 setters
      ∪ Env {tick, price, rate, index (I' ≥ I under A3), ve, gauge mint}
→   = the guarded relations of §3, applied atomically
```

**Eager reference machine M^E.**

- Pools: α_u = (balanceOf, claimable). M^E applies `u ← u(1 − L/S)` and `g_u += r·u/S`.
- Treasury: α = (B·p − F·1e18, F). M^E charges funding exactly once and marks B at the current rate.

## 7. Counterexample search per invariant

Confidence:

- **[bc-c]**: bundle bytecode, re-executed by the coordinator with byte-identical output.
- **[M]**: exact-integer model.

**T1. Holds** [M `nav_polarity`]. 200 000 random actions give 0 dilutions, 0 over-payments and a maximum deficit of 0.999995 wei. Relaxations: inconsistent in-transaction prices (Rv); outside minting (F is read live).

**T2. FAILS under Rv (FX-16)** [bc-c h6].

1. Initially TBT = 1100 and custody = 1000 wrapped, with f = x = 1 100 000.
2. The rate falls from 1.10 to 1.05. The ledger still says 1100 while custody is worth 1050. CR still reads 2.0 and `isUnderCollateral` is false.
3. The xToken holder redeems everything and receives 523.81 wrapped.
4. `redeemFToken(all)` reverts with "function call to a non-contract account" (L9866, strategy = 0). After redeeming 500 000 fToken, **600 000 fToken cannot be redeemed**.

**T3. FAILS at R0, amplified by R1 (FX-02)** [bc-c h5]. Setup: B = 1000e18, F = X = 1e24, p = 2000. A 1% index rise plus one day gives `harvestable` = 1.25e18.

| Run | Final B (e18) | custody − B | Distributed |
|---|---|---|---|
| Control: valid price; one mint harvests | 998.750000001 | 0 | 1.25 |
| `isValid = false`, 5 mints of 1e9 each | 998.750000001 → 993.765664103792032995 | 6.234335901207967005 | 0 |
| then one mint with a valid price | 992.523505913129659261 | 6.234335901207967005 (**stranded**) | 1.242158191662373734 |

*Mechanism, re-read by the coordinator:*

- `harvest` returns at L17415 without capturing the snapshot.
- Each mint persists the virtually deducted state (L17477–L17478 → L9562).
- The snapshot stays old, so every action deducts the funding again.

In the model with k = 20 invalid-price actions, 24.70 base tokens are stranded and xValue falls by $51 838 instead of $2 500. Recovery requires an upgrade (L17399–L17402).

**T4. FAILS (FX-11)** [M]. Charging a +10% index step in one harvest costs 12.5000e18; in two harvests it costs 12.1649e18, i.e. 2.68% less. The charge also depends on the price at poke time (1.2346 / 1.2500 / 1.2397 base at p = 1800 / 2000 / 2200). MWFC pokes every action, so this is second order.

**T5.** The flow property holds to ≤ 87 wei. **CII fails (FX-12)**: holding one manipulated 12 s sample moves the EMA by 7.25% at T = 60 s, 0.79% at 600 s and 0.13% at 3600 s. Nothing in the bundle consumes the EMA; informational.

**T6. FAILS at R0 (FX-07)**, Proven-derived.

1. The last proportional fToken redemption returns `⌊F·B/F⌋ = B`, so B = 0 (L9604, L9617).
2. From then on `leverageRatio` divides by zero (L8229) on every mint and redeem.
3. Re-initialization is blocked by L9770.
4. `isUnderCollateral` returns false, because xNav = 1e18 when B = 0 (L9886).

**M1. Holds (proof).** CR is evaluated at p_max (L9438, L9926) and the cap at p_min (L9462, L9924). Both oracle reads happen within one transaction, with no user-controlled yield point between them.

**M2. FAILS at R1 with A16 relaxed (FX-10)** [M `stability`]. Starting at CR = 1.25 < SR = 1.3:

1. A direct fToken mint reverts.
2. `mintXToken(500 base)`: CR = 1.7455.
3. `mintFToken`, capped at 1485 base: +1 481 287.5 fToken, CR = 1.3004.
4. `redeemXToken(all)`: CR = 1.1008.

Net effect: +1.48M fToken across stability mode, for 47.77 base in fees (3.2%).

**M3. FAILS at R4 (FX-19).** A fee ratio of exactly 1e18 passes `_validateFeeRatio` (L7526) and then panics with 0x12 (division by zero) at L7631.

**P1. FAILS.**

- Quantized (FX-06): the per-user defect is ≤ 9.3e-13 relative with polarity ≤ 0; Ω reaches 2.3e9 wei.
- Structural (FX-13), [bc-c h3-A]: `liquidate(maxAmount = 1e18)` on S = 1e18 + 1 gives x = 1e18, **scale = 0**, product (1, 0, 1e18), **S = 1** and `balanceOf(A) = 0`.
- The epoch-reset window contains 1, 1e6 and 1e9 loss values at S ≈ 1e18, 1e24 and 1e27 respectively.

**P2. Holds** for fToken (exact BDR).

**P3. FAILS (FX-14)** [bc-c h4].

| Step | Loss L | Scale | Product | S |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1e18 − 1e9 − 1 on S = 1e18 | 1e9 | (0, 0, 1e9) | 1000000001 |
| 2 | refill to S = 1e18, then L = 1e18 − 1 | 1 | **(0, 1, 1)** | 1 |

Then D deposits 5e18. `balanceOf(D)`, `claimable(D)`, `withdraw(D)` and `claim(D)` all panic 0x12 (division by zero at L16452 / L17051). Rewards accrued at magnitude 0 add **0** to the integral. After a full wipe that starts a new epoch, D's `claim` and `deposit` **still** panic 0x12. That is a permanent lock.

This is the coordinator's library-level DFP collapse (`mul(encode(0,0,1), 5e8) → (0,1,0)`), now reached through the pool's actual `scale` computation.

**P4. FAILS.** The epoch advances while S = 1 (FX-13), contradicting L16870–L16871.

**P5. FAILS for denormal snapshots (FX-14b)** [bc-c h4-P2].

1. U deposits 1e24 at product (0, 1, 1).
2. `liquidate(1e18)` gives (0, 2, 999 999 000) and `balanceOf(U)` = 999 999e18.
3. A second `liquidate(1e18)` gives (0, 3, 9.99998e17) and **`balanceOf(U)` = 0**, although U's real stake is 9.99998e23.
4. `withdraw` reverts with `WithdrawZeroAmount`.

**P6. FAILS (FX-08, L17151; FX-14, L17051).**

- The checked uint192 product overflows once accrual exceeds `⌊(2¹⁹²−1)/m⌋·S/1e18`. At m = 1e18 that is 6 277.1 tokens at S = 1, 62 771 at S = 10, and 6.28e9 at S = 1e6.
- The overflow is **cumulative**: poking does not help. In [bc-c h3-A′] the 18th six-hourly poke overflows at +4.5 days.

[bc-c h3-B] trace:

1. A 10k FXN/week stream runs while S = 0. The attacker deposits **1 wei**.
2. Five days later, all of these revert with panic 0x11: an honest 1000e18 deposit, the attacker's own claim and withdraw, `checkpoint(0)`, `liquidate`, and `depositReward`.

[bc-c h3-A] reaches the same state from the FX-13 orphan wei, and it persists at +35 days.

Control: an honest deposit on day 4 prevents it. The 1-wei staker then receives 0 FXN, because 0.4 × 1 floors to 0 and the unboosted remainder is re-notified.

`_claimable` itself cannot overflow (shares·ΔI ≤ Σa·1e36). Contrast with K3 (`01-kernel.md`), where L2294 truncates silently instead.

**P7. FAILS.** See FX-01, FX-03b, FX-05, FX-08, FX-14, FX-16 and FX-17.

**B1b. FAILS** in Boostable:

**B2b. FAILS (FX-03)** [bc-c h1, h1c]. `historyIndex = numTotalSupplyHistory` (L16315) points one past the last written entry, so `totalSupplyHistory[3]` = 0 is read (L16564).

**V1. FAILS.**
